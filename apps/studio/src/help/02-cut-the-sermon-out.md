Everything Amplify makes is cut from the sermon, not from the service. So
this is the one step where a few careless seconds cost you for the rest of
the week — a transcript that opens with announcements produces a blog post
about announcements.

## Upload the recording

Open the **Source** room and upload the recording. The whole service is
fine, and normal: worship, notices, offering and all. It is a big file, so
it uploads in parts and keeps going if your connection hiccups.

You can also bring one in from YouTube if the service is already up there.

## Trim it to the sermon

Once the file is up, you get a player and two marks.

1. Scrub to where the preaching actually begins — after the introduction, on the first sentence of the sermon.
2. Press **Set** on *Sermon starts*.
3. Scrub to where it ends — before the altar call or the closing song, wherever the message stops.
4. Press **Set** on *Sermon ends*.
5. Press **Trim**.

The `-5 · -2 · +2 · +5` buttons nudge a mark by that many seconds without
scrubbing, which is how you land on the exact word.

> Give it a second of air at each end. A cut that lands on the first
> syllable sounds like a mistake; a second of room sounds deliberate.

Trimming runs on the worker and takes a few minutes. When it finishes you
have a **sermon** file separate from the service recording, and that is what
everything downstream reads.

## Changing your mind

**Trim it again** is always there. Re-trimming makes a new sermon file; the
transcript and everything written from it stay as they are until you
re-transcribe, so if you move the start you will want to do that too.

## The shortcut

**Check for anything missing** runs every step that has not been done yet —
transcribe, write everything, find the clips — in the right order, without
you standing over it. It skips anything already finished, so it is safe to
press whenever you are not sure what is outstanding.

It is the fastest way through a normal week. The chapters that follow
explain each step so you know what it did and where to disagree with it.
