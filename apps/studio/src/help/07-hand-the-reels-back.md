A **clip** is the moment cut out of the sermon. A **reel** is what an editor
makes from it — captioned, cropped to vertical, titled, ready to post. The
two stay separate on purpose: the clip never changes, which is what lets an
editor work from it and what stops two people building a reel out of the
same moment without noticing.

## The round trip

1. Open the clip and press **Download**. The file is named after the hook, so it arrives in the editor's downloads folder as something they can identify.
2. Edit it wherever your church edits — captions, crop, music, whatever your reels normally have.
3. Come back to the same clip and press **Upload the reel**.

That upload is what makes it a reel. Nothing before it counts, and nothing
else needs pressing.

## What happens on upload

As soon as the reel lands, Amplify writes its **captions** — a title and a
description for Instagram, TikTok, Shorts and Facebook, each one written for
that platform rather than pasted across all four. They appear on the clip a
few seconds later, each with a **Copy** button.

You also get a **cover** for that reel: five vertical concepts, written from
that clip's own words, exactly like the sermon cover but for a phone screen.
Press **Write the cover**, copy the brief you like, make the picture, and
attach it with **Attach the cover**.

> The reel's cover is written from the clip, not from the sermon. A cover
> about the sermon's overall theme sitting on a thirty-second clip about
> something specific is the mistake this avoids.

## Reading the gallery

Each tile carries one badge, showing how far that moment has got. The order
is the order of the work:

- **cut** — there is a file.
- **written** — it has captions.
- **cover on** — a picture is attached, and you can see it inset on the tile.
- **reel ready** — an editor has handed the finished reel back. The tile gets a green ring, because this is the one state worth seeing from across the room.

A tile with no badge is a suggestion nobody has acted on yet.

## Replacing one

**Replace the reel** swaps the video for a new cut of the same moment. It
deliberately leaves the captions alone — if you have already scheduled a
post around them, a re-upload silently rewriting them would be worse than
keeping them. If you do want them rewritten, press **Write them now**.

## What comes next

At this point the sermon has everything it needs: the writing, a cover, and
however many reels the sermon was worth. Publishing — actually sending it
out — is handled by an admin, and is the last chapter.
