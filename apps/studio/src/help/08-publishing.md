Most people never open this room. Preparing a sermon and sending it out are
different jobs: the first is editorial and reversible, the second puts your
church's name somewhere public under an account somebody had to connect.

If you prepare sermons, your work finishes when the reels are handed back.
This chapter is for whoever presses the button.

## What is on the page

The Publish room lists everything this sermon can go out as, in two groups.

**The sermon** — the sermon itself, three ways:

- **YouTube** — the full video, with the title, description and tags from the writing, and the cover you picked.
- **Facebook** — the short written version, with a link to the blog post.
- **Website** — the blog post, with its cover, posted to the church site.

**The reels** — one group per reel, each showing the four platforms it can
go to. Grouped by reel rather than by platform, because a reel is the unit
somebody works in: one clip, edited once, posted to three places.

## Before you send

**Make a link** creates a read-only link to the writing that needs no
account. Send it to whoever preached the sermon and let them read it before
any of it goes out. Without this, the review happens by pasting six things
into a text message, which is how the wrong version gets approved. **Turn it
off** revokes it.

The page will tell you if the transcript was never approved. It does not
stop you — it says so, because everything on the page was written from it.

## Sending

**Post it** sends that one row. The row then reads *posted by Amplify* with
the date, and links to the thing that went out.

**Mark as posted** is for when somebody posted it by hand. It records the
fact without sending anything, so the sermon's history stays honest and the
count at the top of the page stays true.

> A row that will not send says why, once, above the buttons. The reason is
> almost always the file — a reel that is not vertical, or a clip nobody
> cut. It is said once per reel rather than once per platform, because it is
> the same fact three times.

## Connections

A platform can only be posted to if it has been connected, which happens in
**Settings** and only needs doing once per church.

- **YouTube** and **TikTok** connect with a **Connect** button — you sign in to the account and Amplify keeps the token.
- **Website (Wix)**, **Facebook** and **Instagram** need credentials pasted in.

A destination with nothing connected still appears on the page, greyed, so
you can see what is possible rather than wondering why a platform is
missing.

## If something fails

A failed row says what went wrong and stays failed until it is dealt with —
it does not quietly retry. Fix the cause and press it again. Posting the
same thing twice is worse than posting it late, so nothing here happens
without somebody pressing something.
