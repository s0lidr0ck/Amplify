The thumbnail decides whether the sermon gets watched at all. It is the
highest-leverage picture in the whole week, which is why it has a room of
its own rather than sitting at the bottom of a list of prose.

## The concepts

The **Visuals** room holds five cover concepts, written from the sermon. Each
one is lettered **A** to **E** and carries:

- a **phrase** — one to four words, the thing that has to be readable at the size of a postage stamp,
- and a **brief** — the scene, the shot, the light, the colour, the type, the framing, and notes for whoever makes it.

They are written to be different from each other on purpose: different shot
distances, different palettes, different places for the text. If two of them
could be the same photograph with different words on it, that is a bug and
worth telling us about.

## Making the picture

Amplify does not draw the picture. It writes the brief.

1. Read the five and pick the one that fits the sermon.
2. Press **Copy the brief** on that concept.
3. Paste it into whatever image tool your church uses.
4. Bring the finished image back and attach it here.

**Read the detail** opens the full brief if you want to see every field
before copying, or to change something in the image tool afterwards.

## Attaching it

Upload the image, and it sits with the sermon from then on. You can attach
several — three covers for one sermon is not a mistake, it is how you find
out which one works — and **star** the one that is the real cover.

> The website will not publish a sermon without a cover picked. YouTube
> will, but it chooses a frame from the video by itself, and the frame it
> chooses is usually the preacher mid-blink. Pick one.

## Reel covers are separate

The covers here are for the **sermon** — the wide picture on the website and
on YouTube. Each reel gets its own cover, vertical, written from that clip's
own words, and you will find it on the clip rather than here. That is the
next chapter but one.
